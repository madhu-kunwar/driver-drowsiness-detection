## Non-negotiable claim boundaries

- This is a **preliminary feasibility study**, not a deployment-ready safety system.
- The highest accuracy is **57.2% for System B**, but the best macro-F1 is **30.7% for System C**.
- The **1.0 ms p50 and 1.3 ms p95** values describe MobileNetV2 inference, not complete end-to-end latency.
- Critical-class detection is not reliable.
- Three drivers are not sufficient for broad generalization.
- Insurance deployment, regulatory approval, demographic fairness, and legal admissibility were not validated in the submitted paper.
