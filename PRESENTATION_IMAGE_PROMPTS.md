## Practical generation and slide-assembly rules

1. Generate the artwork without the long slide copy. Add titles, bullets, formulas, tables, and citations in PowerPoint/Google Slides as editable text.
2. Keep every generated image in the same dark navy, teal, amber, and restrained-red palette.
3. Use the same driver identity across Slides 1, 2, 4, 11, and 12 if the generator supports reference images.
4. Do not ask an image generator to recreate confusion matrices, ROC curves, formulas, or exact metrics. Use the real project artifacts and editable text.
5. Use no more than one major illustration per slide. The speaker tells the story; the image establishes the scene.
6. Minimum text sizes for a classroom/projector: title 30–36 pt, body 20–24 pt, chart labels at least 18 pt.
7. Add a small source line where appropriate: “Evaluation: DMD, subject-independent split, 3,605 held-out frames.”
